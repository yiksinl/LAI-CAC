What this estimate means

LeafView will apply a deterministic 600-tree XGBoost model to quality-screened GOES-19 reflectance and observation geometry. The training reference is a screened MODIS/VIIRS consensus—not a field measurement. Estimates after April 6, 2026 test the model on later observations; they are not forecasts or plant-health diagnoses.
